# DraBornGo / Last Mile v0.6.1 — Google Play privacy URLs

Use these public HTTPS URLs in Google Play Console:

- App / legal hub: https://www.draborneagle.com/draborngo/lastmile/
- Privacy Policy: https://www.draborneagle.com/draborngo/lastmile/gizlilik/
- Account deletion / data deletion resource: https://www.draborneagle.com/draborngo/lastmile/hesap-silme/

The same Privacy Policy and Account Deletion links are exposed inside the app from **Ayarlar → Gizlilik Politikası ve Hesap Silme**.

The account deletion flow is available both:
1. in-app for an authenticated player, through the JWT-protected `dkd-last-mile-api` `delete_account` action, and
2. externally through the public account-deletion page for users who no longer have the app installed.
